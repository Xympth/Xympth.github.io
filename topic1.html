On-Page SEO

On-page SEO focuses on the content and HTML elements that appear on a webpage. This includes page titles, headings, keywords, internal links, image descriptions, and useful writing.

Good on-page SEO starts with people-first content. A page should clearly answer a user's question instead of only trying to rank for keywords.

Titles

Clear titles help users and search engines understand the page topic.

Headings

Headings break information into sections and make the article easier to scan.

Images

Alt text explains images for accessibility and search understanding.
