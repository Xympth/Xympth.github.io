Key SEO Concepts

SEO includes several connected areas. The strongest websites usually combine helpful content, organized structure, mobile design, and reliable links.

Keywords

Words or phrases users type into search engines.

Search Intent

The reason a user searches, such as learning, buying, or comparing.

Backlinks

Links from other websites that can show trust and authority.

Crawling

Search engines discover pages by following links.

Indexing

Search engines store and organize pages after crawling.

Ranking

Search engines order results based on relevance and usefulness.
